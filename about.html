About Jedopab Consult Limited

A trusted partner for organizations seeking sustainable growth through capability building, strategy, and execution.

Adedotun Oluwatosin

Founder & CEO

Our Foundation

As the founder & Chief Executive Officer, Adedotun Oluwatosin leads our strategic vision. Being a seasoned Human Resource Consultant, Leadership Development Expert, and Training and Development Leader with almost 20years experience across manufacturing, financial services, Agriculture, and Visa & Consular Services, delivering exceptional services in Nigeria and some African countries.

A globally licenced Human Resource Professional with SPHRi certification (HRCi, USA) and other extensive academic and professional qualifications such as BSc, MSc, FIMC, FITD,CMC, and ACIPM, he drives the business vision to profitability, Growth and Sustainable Impact.

Our ambition extends beyond delivering services. We seek to create measurable and sustainable impact by developing people, transforming organizations, strengthening businesses, and contributing to economic and social development.
